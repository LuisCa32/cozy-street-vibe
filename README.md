# Cozy Street Vibe

A quiet autumn street at dusk, in pixel art: leaves drift down and pile up on the sidewalk, smoke curls from the chimneys, windows glow, a black cat wanders by, and a slow lo-fi jazz tune plays along.

Everything is generated by code in a single `index.html`: vanilla JavaScript, Canvas 2D and the Web Audio API, with no libraries, images or audio files. Each seed builds its own street and its own tune, and the same seed always gives the same result.

## Run it

- Open `index.html` in a browser, or
- serve the folder locally, for example `python3 -m http.server 8000`, then visit `http://localhost:8000/`.

To publish it with GitHub Pages, go to **Settings → Pages**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, and pick `main` with the `/ (root)` folder. The page will be at `https://luisca32.github.io/cozy-street-vibe/`.

## Controls

| Input | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Click / tap | A gust of wind, blowing away from where you clicked. The first click also starts the music |
| `H` (or the `⋯` button on phones) | Shows the panel: seed box, leaf count, wind strength, music volume and on/off |
| `R` | New random street |
| `M` | Music on / off |
| `G` or `Space` | A gust |
| `V` | Records a 12-second clip at 1080 px wide, music included, and downloads it |

Browsers only allow sound after you interact with the page, so the music starts on your first click, tap or key press.

## URL options

- `?seed=123` opens a specific street. Words work too: `?seed=rainy`
- `?ratio=9x16` gives a vertical layout for phones and stories; square is the default
- The same options work after `#` for hosts that drop the query string: `#123`, `#9x16`, `#9x16-123`

The address updates as you change seeds, so you can copy it to share a street.

## What's in a street

- A dithered purple dusk sky with a moon and twinkling stars, two layers of distant skyline, and a row of gabled houses. There's one lit café or bookshop under a striped awning
- 2 to 4 trees grown by recursive branching, a street lamp with a bench, porch lights and candle-lit jack-o'-lanterns
- About 220 falling leaves that tumble, sway and ride a noise-driven wind. Every so often a gust sweeps across, shaking leaves off the trees and lifting ones off the ground
- Leaves pile softly on the sidewalk and street, darken and fade, so the piles never overflow
- Window lights switch on and off now and then, with little silhouettes inside: a cat on the sill, a plant, a reading lamp. Warm light spills onto the sidewalk and into the low fog
- A wet street that reflects the lamp, and a slow camera drift that shows the parallax between layers
- No visible start or reset, so any 10–15 second stretch makes a good clip

## The music

A small lo-fi jazz trio, synthesized in the browser:

- electric piano playing smooth 7th and 9th chords
- upright bass, either half-note lines or walking
- brushes, a soft kick and sometimes a ride cymbal
- a vibraphone melody in four-bar phrases
- a little tape wobble, vinyl crackle and room reverb

The seed picks the key, the tempo (62–78 bpm), the swing, one of seven chord progressions, the playing styles and the timbre. The panel shows the result, for example `♪ C minor · 65 bpm`.

The music follows the street:

- a gust brings a panned wind swell and a brushed cymbal
- a window lighting up plays a vibraphone note from the current chord, from that side of the stereo field
- the lamp's flicker briefly muffles the sound
- the cat gets a tiptoeing piano figure when it starts walking, and a soft two-note phrase when it sits down

## Make it yours

Every setting worth changing is in the `CONFIG` object at the top of the script:

| Setting | Controls |
| --- | --- |
| `PALETTE`, `LEAF_COLORS` | The 16 colours, and which of them the leaves use |
| `LEAF_COUNT`, `LEAF_FALL_SPEED`, `LEAF_SWAY_AMP` | How many leaves there are and how they move |
| `PILE_LIFETIME`, `PILE_MAX` | How long fallen leaves stay, and the cap on the pile |
| `WIND_BASE`, `WIND_NOISE`, `WIND_STRENGTH` | The steady breeze and how much it varies |
| `GUST_INTERVAL`, `GUST_STRENGTH` | How often gusts come and how strong they are |
| `WINDOW_LIT_SHARE`, `CAT_INTERVAL`, `LAMP_FLICKER_INTERVAL` | How lively the street is |
| `CAMERA_DRIFT` | The parallax drift (`0` keeps the camera still) |
| `QUANTIZE`, `DITHER` | Snapping every frame to the palette, and how strong the dithering is |
| `MUSIC_VOLUME`, `MUSIC_TEMPO` | Starting volume and tempo range |
| `RECORD_SECONDS`, `RECORD_BITRATE` | Clip length and quality for `V` |

The chord progressions, keys and piano rhythms live in `PROGRESSIONS`, `KEYS` and `COMPING`, in the music section of the script.

## How it works

- The scene is drawn into a small buffer, 216×216 or 180×320 for vertical, and scaled up with smoothing off so the pixels stay crisp. Recordings are exact 5× or 6× nearest-neighbour upscales.
- Each frame is snapped to the 16-colour palette through a colour lookup table. Glows, fog and fading leaves are rendered with 4×4 ordered dithering, which keeps them in pixel-art style.
- A seeded mulberry32 generator builds the scene, drives the motion and writes the music. Before the first frame, the simulation runs 25 seconds ahead so the street is already alive when it appears.
- The music is scheduled a bar at a time. Scene events, such as gusts, lights, the lamp and the cat, trigger extra sounds timed to the moment they happen on screen.

## Browser notes

- On iPhone the ring/silent switch can mute web audio.
- Safari records `.mp4` instead of `.webm`.
- Keep the tab in the foreground while recording.
